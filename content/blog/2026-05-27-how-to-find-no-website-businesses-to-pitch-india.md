## Introduction to Finding Clients
You're a freelance web designer in Jaipur, struggling to find clients. You've tried everything - posting on social media, attending networking events, even asking friends and family for referrals. But nothing seems to be working. You're starting to think that maybe you're just not cut out for this freelancing thing. Don't worry, you're not alone. Many freelancers in India face the same problem. The key is to identify potential clients who need your services. One such group is businesses without websites. They're out there, and they need your help. You just need to know how to find them.

## Identifying Potential Clients
A business without a website is like a shop without a signboard. They're missing out on potential customers. You can help them get online and increase their sales. But first, you need to find them. Start by looking around you. Walk down the streets of Jaipur, and you'll see many small businesses - shops, restaurants, gyms, and more. How many of them have a website? Probably not many. You can also check online directories like Justdial or Sulekha. These websites list businesses in your area, and you can filter them by category. You can also use tools like LocalLeads to find businesses without websites in your area. It's a great way to get started.

## Research and Shortlisting
Once you've identified potential clients, it's time to research and shortlist them. You need to find out if they're interested in getting a website. You can do this by visiting their shop or giving them a call. Introduce yourself, and ask if they have a website. If they don't, ask if they're interested in getting one. This is a crucial step. You don't want to waste your time pitching to businesses that are not interested. You can also check their social media presence. If they have a Facebook page or Instagram account, it's likely they're interested in having an online presence.

## Crafting a Pitch
Now that you've shortlisted potential clients, it's time to craft a pitch. This is where most freelancers go wrong. They either pitch too hard or don't pitch at all. You need to find a balance. Your pitch should be clear, concise, and persuasive. Here's a script you can use:
"Hi, my name is [Your Name], and I'm a freelance web designer from Jaipur. I noticed that your business doesn't have a website. I'd like to offer my services to help you get online. A website can help you increase sales, improve customer engagement, and build your brand. I've worked with similar businesses in the past, and I'm confident I can deliver a high-quality website that meets your needs. My price starts at Rs 20,000 for a basic website. If you're interested, we can discuss the details further."

## Pricing and Negotiation
Pricing is a critical aspect of your pitch. You need to charge enough to make a profit, but not so much that you scare off potential clients. Rs 20,000 is a good starting point for a basic website. You can increase the price based on the complexity of the project. Negotiation is also key. Be prepared to negotiate, but don't compromise on your prices too much. You can offer discounts for long-term contracts or referrals. For example, you can offer a 10% discount if the client signs a 6-month contract.

## Taking Action
You've got the script, now it's time to take action. Start by making a list of 10 potential clients. Visit their shops or give them a call. Introduce yourself, and pitch your services. Don't be discouraged if you don't get a positive response from all of them. Rejection is part of the game. But if you persist, you'll eventually get clients. You can also offer free consultations to potential clients. This is a great way to showcase your expertise and build trust.
